# Privacy

Sheet does not collect, send or share any data. It has no network
permission: the app cannot reach the internet.

- **Camera**: used only while the camera screen is open, to show the
  preview and take the photos you take. Detection of the page and reading
  of the text run on the phone.
- **Motion sensor** (gyroscope, no permission needed): read only while the
  camera screen is open, so that the photo is taken once the phone is
  held still. The readings are not stored.
- **Your document** (photos, straightened pages and their text) stays in
  the app's private storage while you scan and is deleted when you save
  or discard it; sharing it leaves it there.
- **Saved PDFs** go to the one folder you picked; Sheet keeps the
  permission for that folder only, and lets go of it when you pick
  another.
- **Shared PDFs** are handed to the app you choose; Sheet keeps a copy in
  its cache until the next share.

There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting.
